About the device

CLYNORA's wearable is built on an ESP32 microcontroller. This page lists what exists today and what is planned.

Working today

ESP32 board, AD8232 Lead II ECG sensor, MAX30102 optical sensor for pulse and SpO₂, temperature measurement, Web Serial streaming, and transparent rule-based threshold scoring.

Planned

On-device edge anomaly classification, offline non-volatile buffering with automatic sync, caregiver push notifications, and battery telemetry.

Clinical Disclaimer

Values appear only when the wearable sends them or when simulation is active. Decision support tool, not automated diagnosis.

About CLYNORA

CLYNORA is a specialized MedTech platform for continuous remote vital-sign monitoring in general wards, rural clinics, and semi-urban facilities where nurse-to-patient ratios are high. It empowers care teams and does not replace clinical judgement.
